The PCP verifiergt-10-answer-reduction.tex fig:pcpverifierC1 TESTED

Five low-degree extensions, one polynomial that must vanish on the cube, and a verifier that reads a handful of points and lines.

The proof string is Π = (g₁,…,g₅, c₀, c₁,…,c_{m′}): five low-degree extensions of the witness blocks, the composite polynomial, and its quotients. The verifier picks a random point, restricts the proof to axis-parallel and diagonal lines through it, checks degrees, and then checks two algebraic identities — the formula test and the zero-on-subcube test. Everything below is measured on the six-gate instance.

the budget, honestly

Witness (i) has 33,432 monomials in c₀; the five per-block products cost 37,240 / 33,432 / 33,432 / 33,432 / 33,432, so the cumulative 170,968 exceeds the 160,000-monomial budget while the peak 37,240 does not. Failed hypotheses are printed as FAIL and unmeasured costs as NOT_EVALUABLE; neither is ever folded into a PASS. That discipline is what makes the toy-regime chapters below readable at all.

What C1 is and is not

It is completeness evidence on named points and lines, replayed node by node from the constructor's own sub-terms, with an object-identity check so a certificate borrowed from another proof is refused with :certificate_binding. It is not an exhaustive all-z statement and not a soundness claim. No uniform z in GF(2¹¹)¹⁶ are sampled — that gap is tracked as an open issue.

Three roles times eighteen question types is 2,916 ordered pairs — and on 2,736 of them the decider is required to check nothing at all.

Introspectiongt-08-introspection.texC14 CONJECTURE

The verifier stops sending questions and instead asks the provers to sample their own — which only works because entanglement lets it check that they sampled honestly.

designed, not yet executable

Everything in this chapter is transcribed into docs/DESIGN.md §11 with source line references, and nothing in it has run. It is here because it is the piece that makes compression exponential: a verifier of index n would need 2n-bit questions, so instead the provers are made to generate their own questions from shared randomness, and the verifier checks the generation with a Pauli test.

The fuel gate, and why it is not a formalitygt-08-introspection.tex:417–419

Every call the introspection decider makes into the input verifier — sampler or decider, including Dimension(N) — gets exactly R = Nλ units, and the counter rejects before executing step R+1; a return at step R is permitted. λ-boundedness is two separate conditions — a byte-size bound on |V| and a runtime bound — and neither discharges the other. The design records that every child-call record must carry fixture, quote hash, mode, role, stage, exact metered cost, the source R, the supplied budget, and the timeout/return; until a quoted-interpreter trace exists, costs stay NOT_EVALUABLE(owner=tb6-child-meter) and no dependent acceptance may pass.

The Pauli decider's predicates are ordinary field, polynomial-evaluation, parity and equality calls, so they would be executable; quantum rigidity — the actual content of thm:pauli — is not part of that predicate and remains CITED.

Anchoring and repetitiongt-11-parallel-repetition.texC13 CONJECTURE

Compression costs soundness; parallel repetition buys it back — and the toy diagnostic in this repo shows why naive repetition cannot.

designed, not yet executable

Each stage of Compress degrades the soundness gap, so the last stage restores it by running k(n) copies in parallel. Anchoring comes first: a second question type whose only legal answer is the canonical bit 0, which gives the repetition theorem the structure it needs.

The diagnostic that rules out the naive routeC6, N1 — both PROVED

The one thing on this page with a proved status is a negative result. A recursive midpoint protocol verifies y = f2ⁿ(x) in O(n) verifier time and has perfect completeness — but for a false claim the optimal acceptance probability is exactly 1 − 2−n, and adaptive r-copy sequential repetition gives exactly (1 − 2−n)r. So amplification needs Θ(2ⁿ) repetitions and destroys the compression. That is the precise sense in which an iteratable, gap-preserving transformation is the hard requirement.

C6 and N1 are PROVED via verdicts/midpoint-r2.md, with 1,380 exact values checked over ℤ/5 and friends. The verdict struck the stronger phrasing "cannot serve as a compression step" (objection O15) — the negative result is about sequential repetition.

A source inconsistency the code refuses to smooth over

gt-12:70 prints k(n) = (λn)τ inside ComputeParrepVerifier, conflicting with gt-11:200 and gt-12:355, which both say (λn)(1+c′)τ. The description retains the latter two and reports the inconsistent line without using it. The source also calls k(n) an integer while declaring only c′ > 0; production keeps the symbolic term plus an ASSUMED integrality witness rather than silently rounding.
